## Direction
Collective consciousness made tangible. The hero leads on the feeling, not a service menu.

**Hero line:** "We make the sound people feel before they can name it."
**Sub:** Songs, ad music, and jingles, made whole — composed, written, arranged, produced, with mix and master built in. Fifteen years. One collective.

## Positioning
Production is the hero. Mix and master ride inside every build, standalone only when that's all a client needs. Reframed the live "One Stop Audio" line as "music made whole."

## Services (6 cards, all production-first)
1. Songs, start to finish — composition, songwriting (Hindi/Urdu/English), arrangement, vocal production. *Mix + master included.*
2. Ad music & jingles — brief to broadcast, brand/film/campaign. *Mix + master included.*
3. Production & collaboration — demo-to-record, session work, overflow capacity. *Mix + master included.*
4. Mixing & mastering — standalone service, and the finish line of every build above.
5. Music business consultancy — splits, work-for-hire, PRO royalties, release, distribution.
6. Studio access & the room — Space Cat (synths/outboard, Tamzid) + Madbus (guitars/bass, Cyril).

## Design system
- **Aesthetic:** 35mm-filmic. Film-grain overlay, deep-ink + warm amber, ember accent.
- **Palette:** ink #0d0f14 / ink-2 #14171f / paper #f3ece1 / amber #e0a24a / ember #d1573b.
- **Type:** Fraunces (display) · Space Grotesk (body) · IBM Plex Mono (utility/labels).
- **Signature element:** live scrolling credits ticker — Sony, Warner, Universal India, A.R. Rahman, Netflix title track, T-Series, Nippon India, Bajaj Broking — proof in motion, pauses on hover.
- **Sections:** hero → credits ticker → manifesto → services → proof (stats + credits) → process (brief/make/finish/deliver) → the collective (rooms) → CTA band → contact form → footer.
