Why we exist

A collective is not a company. It is a family of producers, composers, lyricists, singers, and engineers who make music that says what people feel but haven't found the words for.

We started in Andheri, Mumbai, and we build the same way whether the brief is a single, a film cue, or a thirty-second spot: idea first, ego last, everyone in the room.
What we make

Music, made whole.

How it works

From voice note to master.

One team the whole way through. No handoffs to strangers, no telephone game, no piece of your record made by someone who never heard the brief.

01

Brief

Tell us the feeling, the reference, the deadline. A demo, a lyric, a mood, or a blank page. We start from wherever you are.

02

Make

We compose, write, and produce. You hear a first pass early and shape it with us, not after us.

03

Finish

Mix and master built into the same build. The record leaves sounding like it belongs on air.

04

Deliver

To spec, on time, with the files and clearances you need to release, air, or pitch it forward.
